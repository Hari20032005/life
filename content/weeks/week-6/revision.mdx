## Week 6 revision notes

<Callout kind="keypoints">

- Ask about the problem before the solution; capture the client's words and numbers; get sign-off.
- Success criteria are measurable with a baseline and a measurement method.
- Every component has a one-line reason; name each SPOF and its mitigation; cost estimates use measured inputs.
- Small PRs, tests, "How I verified this", explain-back; keep main deployable.
- Change requests: show the trade-off, offer options, recommend, decide in writing.
- Launch only with evidence: security checks, evals, backups restored, monitoring alerting, rollback rehearsed.
- Be sceptical of AI patches: edited tests and weakened checks are red flags.
- Demo against success criteria; hand over docs someone else can follow; be honest about AI mistakes.

</Callout>
